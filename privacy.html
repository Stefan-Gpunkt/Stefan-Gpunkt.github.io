Privacy Policy – NAS Backup

NAS Backup is a private tool used only by the members of one household to back up their own data.

  • Access is limited to the Google Drive scope drive.file: the tool can only see and manage files it created itself.
  • Data is encrypted on the NAS before it is uploaded and is stored only in the user's own Google Drive.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or transferred to any third party. No analytics or tracking are used.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
